EARTH WALLPAPER FOR macOS — INSTALLATION

This beta provides separate packages for Apple Silicon (arm64, M1 or newer) and
Intel (x64) Macs with macOS 13 or newer. Use the DMG matching the Mac's processor.
It is ad-hoc signed but is not notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper may block its
first launch even though the application bundle has passed its integrity checks.

1. Drag "Earth Wallpaper.app" from this disk image to Applications.
2. In Applications, Control-click Earth Wallpaper and choose Open.
3. If prompted, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
4. If macOS still does nothing, open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Earth Wallpaper.app"
   open "/Applications/Earth Wallpaper.app"

The xattr command removes the internet-download quarantine attribute from this
application only. It does not disable Gatekeeper or change system-wide security.

Earth Wallpaper runs as a menu-bar application and may not show an icon in the Dock.
